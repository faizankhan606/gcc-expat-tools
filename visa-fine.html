    UAE overstay fines

    • The fine is AED 50 per day for tourist, visit and residence visas.
    • After a residence visa is cancelled or expires, most people get a grace period before fines start. Thirty days is the common figure; Golden and Green visa holders and some skilled categories get longer.
    • Treat tourist and visit visas as having no grace period. The fine starts the day after the visa ends.
    • Service and admin fees are added at payment.

    Example

    A residence visa is cancelled on 1 March and the holder leaves on 20 April. That is 50 days after cancellation. With a 30-day grace period there are 20 overstay days, so the fine is 20 × AED 50 = AED 1,000.
