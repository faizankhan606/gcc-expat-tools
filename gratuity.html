    How UAE gratuity is calculated

    The UAE rules come from Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, which has applied to private-sector jobs since February 2022.

    • You need at least one full year of continuous service.
    • You earn 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years.
    • You earn 30 days of basic wage for each year after five.
    • Part of a year counts in proportion, once you have passed the first year.
    • The daily wage is your last basic monthly salary divided by 30.
    • The total cannot be more than two years of wage.
    • Days of unpaid leave are not counted as service.

    Resigning no longer reduces the amount. The old law cut gratuity for people who resigned early; the current law pays the same rates whether you resign or are let go.

    Example

    Basic salary AED 5,000, service 7 years. Daily wage is 5,000 ÷ 30 = AED 166.67. First five years: 5 × 21 × 166.67 = AED 17,500. Next two years: 2 × 30 × 166.67 = AED 10,000. Total gratuity: AED 27,500.

    How Kuwait indemnity is calculated

    Kuwait's rules are in Labour Law No. 6 of 2010, Articles 51 and 53. This calculator covers monthly-paid workers, which is how most expat employees are paid.

    • You earn 15 days of wage for each of the first five years.
    • You earn one month of wage for each year after five.
    • The calculation uses your last full wage, including regular allowances, not only the basic.
    • The daily wage is normally the monthly wage divided by 26 working days.
    • The total cannot be more than one and a half years of wage.
