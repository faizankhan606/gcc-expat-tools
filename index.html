How these tools work

Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. The only outside request is for the day's public exchange rate, and you can type your own rate instead.

Each calculator page explains the rule it uses and links to the official ministry, so you can check the result yourself.

GCC Expat Calculator gives estimates for general information only. It is not legal, immigration or financial advice, and it is not affiliated with any government body.
